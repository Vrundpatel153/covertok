SECTION 01

Eligibility & 5-Day Guarantee

We have a 5-day replacement policy, which means you have 5 calendar days after receiving your item to request an exchange or replacement.

To be eligible for a return or replacement, your item must meet the following conditions:

  • The product must be in the same condition that you received it, unworn or unused, with tags, protective film, and in its original retail packaging.
  • You must provide the original order confirmation number or invoice provided at checkout.
  • The replacement request must be submitted within 5 days of delivery confirmation from the courier partner.
SECTION 02

Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Items

Please inspect your order immediately upon reception. If the item is defective, damaged in transit, or if you receive the incorrect iPhone cover model or watch band size, contact us immediately so that we can evaluate the issue and make it right.

Crucial Notice: Unboxing Video Policy

To protect both customers and Covertok against transit tampering or mishandling by courier personnel, an uncut unboxing video recorded from the moment the outer courier parcel is sealed is highly recommended. This enables our team to approve an instant replacement without requiring an extended courier investigation.

SECTION 04

Refunds & Cancellation Process

We will notify you once we’ve received and inspected your return, and let you know if the refund was approved or not.

  • Prepaid Orders: If approved, you’ll be automatically refunded on your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days. Please remember it can take some time for your bank or credit card company to process and post the refund too.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: Refunds for verified COD returns are transferred securely via direct bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS) or UPI upon verification of customer bank details.
  • Order Cancellations: You may cancel your order free of cost before it has been dispatched from our warehouse. Since prepaid orders ship within 2 hours, please message us immediately on WhatsApp for cancellation requests.
SECTION 05

Non-Returnable Items & Exceptions

Certain types of items cannot be returned:

  • Custom-printed products or personalised monogram phone cases, unless there is a verifiable spelling or manufacturing fault caused by Covertok.
  • Items showing obvious signs of customer wear, artificial drop damage, or missing original retail boxes.
  • Free promotional gift items or complimentary charms included during special sale events.

CHAPTER 01

The Covertok Origin

Born from an obsession with industrial design and expressive fashion, Covertok started with a straightforward realization: smartphone owners had to choose between bulky, ugly armor cases or flimsy, cheap aesthetic covers that shattered on first impact.

We set out to eliminate that compromise. By combining aerospace-grade CNC aluminium frames, optical clarity Bayer polycarbonates, and tactile vegan leathers, we built a luxury lifestyle brand tailored for contemporary creators and modern tastemakers across India.

Our Core Philosophy

Your phone is your constant companion. It records your memories, runs your business, and reflects your personality. It deserves protection that feels as luxurious as the device itself.

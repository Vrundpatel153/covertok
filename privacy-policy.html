SECTION 01

Information We Collect

When you visit Covertok, we collect certain information about your device, your interaction with the site, and information necessary to process your purchases:

  • Order Information: When you make a purchase or attempt to make a purchase, we collect your name, billing address, shipping address, payment confirmation reference, email address, and phone number.
  • Device Information: We automatically collect information about your web browser, IP address, time zone, and cookies installed on your device to optimize image loading and mobile responsiveness.
  • Customer Support Conversations: Records of WhatsApp messages or emails sent to our support desk to assist with tracking and warranty claims.
SECTION 02

How We Use Your Personal Information

We use the Order Information that we collect generally to fulfill any orders placed through the Site (including processing your payment, arranging for courier delivery, and providing you with invoices and order confirmations). Additionally, we use this information to:

  • Communicate with you regarding shipping updates, delivery confirmations, and dispatch tracking links.
  • Screen our orders for potential risk, fraud, or incorrect shipping postal codes.
  • When in line with the preferences you have shared with us, provide you with exclusive discount codes and new release announcements.
SECTION 03

Sharing Personal Information with Partners

We only share your Personal Information with vetted third parties strictly necessary to operate our business and fulfill your delivery:

  • Courier & Logistics Partners: We share your recipient name, delivery address, and phone number with BlueDart, Delhivery, DTDC, and Ekart solely to execute physical door delivery.
  • Payment Gateways: Payment details (UPI handles, card transactions) are processed through RBI-authorized payment processors with PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance. Covertok does not store raw credit card numbers.
SECTION 04

Cookies & Local Storage

We use cookies and browser local storage to maintain your active shopping cart, remember your preferred theme or device filter, and ensure seamless navigation between product views and checkout.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. However, disabling cookies may prevent your cart items from persisting across page refreshes.

SECTION 05

Data Retention & Security Safeguards

When you place an order through Covertok, we maintain your Order Information for our records unless and until you ask us to delete this information.

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, destruction, alteration, or disclosure.
